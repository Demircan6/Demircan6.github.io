Snugo Mini Games Privacy Policy / Gizlilik Politikası

Last updated / Son güncelleme: 7 November 2026 · Publisher / Yayımlayan: Barış Demircan

English

In short. Snugo has no account and no sign-in. Your progress and settings are saved on your phone. The game shows ads from Google AdMob and sends usage statistics and crash reports to Firebase, also by Google — in the UK, the European Economic Area and Switzerland, ads only after you answer Google's consent form, and statistics and crash reports only if you agree. To sell Premium and check whether it stands, the game uses RevenueCat under a random identifier. It never asks for your name, email, contacts, photos or location.

What stays on your phone. Your save — scores, Adventure progress, daily puzzles, the statistics screen and settings — lives in the game's own storage on the device and is never uploaded by the game. Android's cloud backup is switched off for Snugo, so it is not copied to your Google account either; on iPhone it is part of your own iCloud backup if you use one. Uninstalling the game deletes it. The usage statistics below include a few of the same numbers (a Classic game's score and length, which level you finished), sent as events, not your save.

Advertising (Google AdMob). Snugo is free and paid for by ads: a banner at the bottom of the screen while you play, a full-screen ad when a game is lost or a level ends, and a rewarded video you can choose to watch for a second chance. Premium takes them away. To show and measure ads, Google's Mobile Ads SDK collects: your device's advertising ID and app set ID (on iPhone, the advertising identifier only if you allow tracking, and the identifier for vendors), your IP address (from which an approximate location may be derived), and information about ads you see and tap and about the app's performance. Google uses this as described at https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites. Google may let other ad networks and buyers bid to fill an ad space; to do so they receive the same kinds of data through Google (IP address, the advertising ID where allowed, device and ad information). In the UK, the EEA and Switzerland they are listed by name in Google's consent form. Ads in Snugo are limited to content suitable for a general audience.

Your consent choices. In the UK, the European Economic Area and Switzerland, Snugo shows Google's consent form before any ad is requested, and you decide whether ads may be personalised and whether data may be stored on your device. You can change your answer at any time in Settings → Privacy choices. In US states whose privacy laws let you opt out of the sale or sharing of personal information for targeted advertising, Settings → Privacy choices opens Google's form, where you can opt out of personalised ads. On iPhone the game asks for your permission before the advertising identifier is used (Apple's App Tracking Transparency); if you say no, ads still show but are not based on it, and you can change your answer in the iPhone's Settings. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in your phone's settings.

Statistics and crash reports (Firebase). In the UK, the EEA and Switzerland only with your consent, and elsewhere by default, the game sends Firebase Analytics five events of its own — which mode you start, a Classic game's score and length, which Adventure level you start and whether you finished it, and how a revive offer was answered — plus Firebase's standard automatic events (first open, sessions, engagement time, screens viewed, app and OS updates, clearing the app's data, uninstalling, and app errors) and, once the owner links AdMob to Firebase, ad impression events; and Firebase Crashlytics a report if the game crashes or hits an error, including when your progress could not be saved (the error, device model and OS version). These are tied to a random installation identifier and an approximate location derived from your IP address, never to your name or contacts; the game does not collect your advertising ID for statistics and never uses them for advertising. Google processes them as described at https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy.

Reminders and notifications. After your first finished game Snugo asks whether it may remind you; nothing is asked before, and if you say no it never asks again. If you say yes and allow notifications on your phone, the game schedules a few reminders on the phone itself when you leave it — at most two a day, fewer and fewer, none after three weeks — and removes them when you come back. They are written from your own save (for example your record or the level you are on); nothing about them is sent anywhere. With your yes, the game also registers with Firebase Cloud Messaging, by Google, so the publisher can occasionally send a message to all players or to the players of your language: Google receives a registration token tied to the Firebase installation ID and the topics "all" and your language. Before your yes no token exists. Switching Settings → Notifications off removes the reminders and unregisters the token; you can also switch notifications off in your phone's settings.

Premium (the store and RevenueCat). If you take out the Premium subscription, Google Play or Apple's App Store takes the payment; the game never sees your card or bank details, and you manage or cancel the subscription in that store. The game sells Premium and checks whether it stands through RevenueCat, Inc. When the game opens, and when you buy or restore Premium, RevenueCat receives a random app user ID made on your phone, your locale and currency code, the version of the game and of your phone's operating system, your IP address as with any connection, and, if you have ever bought Premium, your purchase history for this game: the product, its price and currency and the store's record of the purchase. RevenueCat uses these to check the purchase with the store and for the game's sales reports, as described at https://www.revenuecat.com/privacy. No name, e-mail or advertising ID goes with them. This is needed for the subscription to work, so it does not depend on the consent choices above.

Offline. Without a connection the game plays fully and shows no ads. Statistics and crash reports recorded meanwhile wait on the phone and are sent the next time it is online.

Permissions. Internet and network state (for ads and statistics); the advertising ID and Android's privacy-preserving advertising APIs (Ad Services: advertising ID, attribution and topics, used by Google's ads SDK where the phone offers them); the Play install referrer (which store page led to the install, used for ad and statistics measurement); wake lock and foreground service (used by Google's SDKs to finish a network request); Google Play billing (for Premium); vibration (for haptic feedback you can switch off in Settings); notifications, asked only after your first finished game and only if you say yes on the game's card; running at the phone's start, so planned reminders survive a restart; and receiving Firebase Cloud Messaging messages (only after your yes). On iPhone the game asks for App Tracking Transparency, described above, and for notifications on the same terms.

Children. Snugo is a general-audience puzzle and is not directed at children under 13.

Retention and deletion. On-device data is yours and is deleted with the game. Firebase keeps event-level statistics for 2 months and crash reports for 90 days. There is no account, so nothing ties them to your name; withdrawing consent or uninstalling stops them. RevenueCat keeps purchase records for the game's sales reports; write to the contact address below with the order number from your receipt and they are deleted.

Who is responsible. Barış Demircan, who publishes Snugo Mini Games, is the data controller and decides what the game collects. RevenueCat processes purchase records on the publisher's behalf. Google and the ad partners it works with are responsible for their own processing as described in Google's policies linked above.

Legal basis. In the UK, the European Economic Area and Switzerland, your consent, given or refused in Google's form and changeable in Settings → Privacy choices. In Türkiye, under KVKK article 5(2): the performance of the contract you enter into by playing or buying (c), and the publisher's legitimate interest in keeping a free game running, paying for it with ads and finding its faults, as long as this does not harm your fundamental rights and freedoms (f). Elsewhere, the same reasons under the law of your region. Outside the consent regions the game has no in-game switch for statistics and crash reports; uninstalling the game stops them.

Your rights. Data protection law gives you rights over data about you — to know what is processed, to have it corrected or deleted, to object, to withdraw consent — and to complain to your data protection authority (in Türkiye, the Personal Data Protection Board — Kişisel Verileri Koruma Kurulu; in the UK, the EEA and Switzerland, your country's supervisory authority). To use them, write first to the contact address below. In Türkiye, under KVKK article 11 you may learn whether data about you is processed, ask for information about it, learn its purpose and whether it is used for that purpose, know who it goes to in Türkiye or abroad, have it corrected if it is incomplete or wrong, have it deleted or destroyed under article 7, have those it went to told of this, object to a result against you that comes only from automated analysis, and claim compensation for damage from unlawful processing. Under article 13 you apply in writing to the contact address below and are answered free of charge within 30 days at the latest. If your application is refused, the answer falls short or none comes in time, under article 14 you may complain to the Board within 30 days of the answer and in any case within 60 days of your application. Snugo has no account: the developer holds nothing that names you and cannot look up or delete the statistics tied to an installation's random identifier; Firebase deletes them on its own schedule (above). In the UK, the EEA and Switzerland, withdrawing consent in Settings → Privacy choices stops statistics, crash reports and ad personalisation; everywhere, uninstalling the game stops all collection. Resetting or deleting your advertising ID in your phone's settings separates future ads from the old ID but does not affect statistics. For anything else, write to the contact address below.

International transfers. Google processes ad and Firebase data, and RevenueCat purchase records, on servers outside your country, including in the United States. For the UK, the EEA and Switzerland these transfers rest on GDPR article 46 safeguards: Google's EU-US Data Privacy Framework certification and standard contractual clauses, and RevenueCat's standard contractual clauses with their UK addendum and Swiss amendments. For Türkiye they rest on the appropriate safeguards of KVKK article 9, through those companies' own terms.

Telefonunuzda kalanlar. Kaydınız — skorlar, Macera ilerlemesi, günlük bulmacalar, istatistik ekranı ve ayarlar — cihazdaki oyunun kendi depolamasında durur ve oyun onu hiçbir yere yüklemez. Snugo için Android'in bulut yedeklemesi kapalıdır, bu yüzden Google hesabınıza da kopyalanmaz; iPhone'da, kullanıyorsanız kendi iCloud yedeğinizin içindedir. Oyunu kaldırmak onu siler. Aşağıdaki kullanım istatistikleri bu sayılardan birkaçını içerir (bir Klasik oyunun skoru ve uzunluğu, bitirdiğiniz seviye); kaydınız değil, olay olarak gönderilir.

Reklamlar (Google AdMob). Snugo ücretsizdir ve reklamlarla desteklenir: oynarken ekranın altında bir banner, oyun kaybedildiğinde ya da bir seviye bittiğinde tam ekran bir reklam ve ikinci şans için izlemeyi seçebileceğiniz ödüllü bir video. Premium bunları kaldırır. Reklam göstermek ve ölçmek için Google Mobile Ads SDK şunları toplar: cihazınızın reklam kimliği ve uygulama seti kimliği (iPhone'da reklam tanımlayıcısı yalnızca izlemeye izin verirseniz, bir de satıcı tanımlayıcısı), IP adresiniz (yaklaşık konum çıkarılabilir) ve gördüğünüz ve dokunduğunuz reklamlar ile uygulamanın performansı hakkında bilgi. Google bunları https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites adresinde anlatıldığı gibi kullanır. Google, bir reklam alanını doldurmak için başka reklam ağlarının ve alıcıların teklif vermesine izin verebilir; bunun için bu ağlar Google aracılığıyla aynı türde verileri (IP adresi, izin verilen yerlerde reklam kimliği, cihaz ve reklam bilgileri) alır. Birleşik Krallık, AEA ve İsviçre'de Google'ın izin formunda adlarıyla listelenirler. Snugo'daki reklamlar genel izleyiciye uygun içerikle sınırlıdır.

İzin seçimleriniz. Cevabınızı istediğiniz zaman Ayarlar → Gizlilik seçenekleri bölümünden değiştirebilirsiniz. Haklarınız. Bu hakları kullanmak için önce aşağıdaki adrese yazın. Madde 13 uyarınca başvurunuzu aşağıdaki adrese yazılı olarak yaparsınız; başvurunuz en geç 30 gün içinde ücretsiz sonuçlandırılır. Birleşik Krallık, AEA ve İsviçre'de Ayarlar → Gizlilik seçenekleri'nden rızayı geri almak istatistikleri, çökme raporlarını ve reklam kişiselleştirmesini durdurur; her yerde oyunu kaldırmak tüm toplamayı durdurur. Reklam kimliğinizi telefonunuzun ayarlarından sıfırlamak veya silmek gelecekteki reklamları eski kimlikten ayırır, ama istatistikleri etkilemez. Diğer her soru için aşağıdaki adrese yazın.
